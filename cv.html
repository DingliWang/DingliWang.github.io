Research Papers

Robust Replacement of Human Decision Makers under Covariate Shift Job Market Paper · Revise and resubmit, Journal of Econometrics I study when an algorithm should replace a human decision maker if the deployment environment differs from the training environment.
Median-Anchored Adjustment of Joint VaR–ES Forecasts with Tae-Hwy Lee Under review, Journal of Business & Economic Statistics A one-parameter adjustment to existing tail-risk forecasts that rescales their distances from the fitted median and preserves VaR–ES ordering.
Adversarial Density Forecast for Macro-financial Risks with Tae-Hwy Lee An anchored conditional Wasserstein generative model for density forecasting in short macroeconomic samples.
When Should Forecasts Be Equally Weighted? Signal Recovery and Estimation Costs A comparison of equal and estimated forecast weights that separates recovered predictive signal from estimation costs.
